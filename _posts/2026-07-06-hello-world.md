They say it is never too late, welcome to my blog!


## Why a Blog? 

I think that, especially in academia, _writing_ is extremely important at all levels.
Despite academic writing being a skill on its own, the underlying purpose of crystallizing thoughts while being minimal and highly informative is extremely fascinating to me.

On a side note, my decision to have a blog is to force myself to write more, and improve my writing skills!

## Content ideas

I plan to write about:

1. **Research**: research directions I find very interesting, about the works I read, reflections on my own work;
2. **Technical results**: algorithms, and proofs that I truly find amazing;
3. **General thoughts**: it could be academic life, or more broad topics 🙂
